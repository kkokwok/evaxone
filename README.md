# Infection Prevention in Residential Care Homes for the Elderly: Insights from the Antibody Landscape of COVID-19 after an Explosive Outbreak

## Overview

Residential Care Homes for the Elderly (RCHEs) were among the most severely affected settings during Hong Kong's territory-wide Omicron BA.2 surge in early 2022. 
Understanding the resulting immunity landscape is critical for informing infection prevention and control strategies in these outbreak-prone environments.

This study investigated the immunologic landscape of COVID-19 among 676 RCHE residents through a cross-sectional seroepidemiological survey conducted in Hong Kong in 2022. 
By integrating serological testing results with residents' vaccination and infection histories, the study examined how different immunologic exposures shaped neutralizing antibody responses following the Omicron outbreak.

## Key Findings

- The median neutralizing antibody (nAb) inhibition against Omicron BA.2 was **46.7%** overall.
- Population immunity increased rapidly following the outbreak, with median inhibition levels rising from **8.63% in February 2022** to **54.1% in April 2022**, before stabilizing through November 2022.
- Residents with **hybrid immunity** (vaccination plus infection) demonstrated substantially higher nAb levels than individuals with vaccination-only or infection-only immunity.
- Generalized additive regression analysis showed that recent infection following vaccination was associated with stronger neutralizing antibody responses than earlier infection profiles, even after accounting for the time elapsed since the last immunologic exposure.
